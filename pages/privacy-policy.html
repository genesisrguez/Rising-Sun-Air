Privacy Policy

Rising Sun Air (“Rising Sun Air,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of customers, website visitors, and people who contact us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information provided through www.risingsunair.com and our business interactions.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide when you:

  • Request an HVAC service, estimate, or appointment;
  • Complete a contact or booking form;
  • Contact us by phone, email, text message, or another channel;
  • Provide information about a home, business, HVAC system, or service issue; or
  • Otherwise communicate or do business with us.

Depending on your interaction, this information may include your name, phone number, email address, service address, preferred appointment time, details about your HVAC equipment or service needs, communications you send us, and information related to a service request. If a payment is made, payment details may be handled by the payment processor used for that transaction.

Our website or service providers may also collect technical information such as browser or device data and website usage information, where those tools are enabled.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to questions and service requests;
  • Schedule, confirm, and provide HVAC services, estimates, or appointments;
  • Send service-related updates and follow up on your request;
  • Maintain business, service, and transaction records;
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website, services, and customer experience; and
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements and protect our rights and operations.

3. How We Share Personal Information

Rising Sun Air does not sell or rent personal information.

We may share information with service providers who support functions such as website hosting, online booking, communications, payment processing, customer service, or other business operations. These providers may use the information only as needed to perform services for us or as otherwise permitted by law.

We may also disclose information when required by law or legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, safety, customers, or operations of Rising Sun Air.

4. Information Submitted Through Forms

Information submitted through forms on our website may be used to respond to your inquiry, arrange an estimate or appointment, provide requested services, and communicate with you about your request.

5. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided or request that it be corrected or updated, subject to applicable legal and business recordkeeping requirements. You may also opt out of marketing communications as described in the message you receive. Service-related communications may still be sent when needed to respond to a request or provide a service.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may use third-party services to support website functionality, online booking, communications, analytics, payment processing, or other operations. For example, booking links may direct you to a third-party scheduling service. Those services handle information under their own privacy terms. Our website may also link to other websites; Rising Sun Air is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, availability, or content of independent third-party websites.
